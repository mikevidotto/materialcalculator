Privacy Policy for JW Sheds

Effective Date: March 27, 2026   Last Updated: March 27, 2026

At JW Sheds, we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our customers' personal information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and disclose your information when you visit our website or make an inquiry about our products.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an inquiry through our website, contact us via email, or call us, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Identity Data: Your full name.
  • Contact Data: Your email address and phone number.
  • Location Data: Your city, province, or postal code to provide accurate delivery quotes and service availability.
  • Inquiry Details: Information you provide regarding shed dimensions, requirements, and site location.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect strictly for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide quotes.
  • To schedule deliveries or site visits.
  • To communicate updates regarding your order or inquiry.
  • To comply with legal or regulatory requirements.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. Consent

By providing your information through our inquiry forms, you provide implied consent for us to use that information to fulfill your request. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us, though this may limit our ability to provide services to you.

4. Disclosure of Information

We only share your information with third parties in limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Where a contractor or installer requires necessary location or contact details to fulfill an order.
  • Legal Requirements: Where disclosure is required by Canadian law or a valid court order.

5. Data Retention and Security

  • Retention: We keep information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected.
  • Safeguards: We use commercially reasonable physical, technical, and administrative safeguards to protect your data from unauthorized access or theft.

6. Your Rights (Access and Correction)

Under PIPEDA, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, request corrections, and request deletion subject to legal record-keeping requirements.

7. Quebec Residents (Law 25 Compliance)

If you are a resident of Quebec, you may have additional rights, including data portability and the right to be informed of automated processing where applicable.
